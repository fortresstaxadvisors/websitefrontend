# Fortress Tax Advisors

## Service Completion Acknowledgment - Sandbox Testing Draft

> **SANDBOX TESTING ONLY - NOT FOR PRODUCTION OR CLIENT USE.** This workflow fixture has not been reviewed or approved by legal counsel. It must not be sent to a real client.

### 1. Purpose and completion record

Fortress Tax Advisors is asking the Client to review the service or milestone record listed below and choose one response. This acknowledgment is intended to preserve a clear record of delivery and the Client's response. It does not replace the signed engagement agreement, any approved amendment, or the professional standards and law applicable to the engagement.

The listed deliverables should describe only the work actually delivered. They should not include Social Security numbers, taxpayer-identification numbers, bank or card information, passwords, full tax returns, or other sensitive tax records.

### 2. What an acknowledgment means

If the Client selects **I acknowledge receipt and review**, the Client confirms only that the listed deliverables were received or made available and that the Client had an opportunity to review them against the stated service or milestone. The response does not state that a taxing authority accepted a filing or position, that every future outcome is known, or that the Client has no questions or concerns.

If the Client selects **I am reporting an issue**, Fortress will treat the description as a request for follow-up. Reporting an issue through this form does not automatically cancel the engagement, reverse or refund a payment, mark an invoice paid, or resolve the issue. Fortress will contact the Client through a verified channel.

### 3. Payment and preserved rights

No card, bank account, check, or other payment is collected or authorized by signing this acknowledgment. Signing does not change the amount or status of an invoice.

Nothing in this acknowledgment waives or restricts a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be waived or restricted. In particular, this acknowledgment does not waive any applicable statutory right, card-network or bank-payment dispute right, right to contact the payment provider, or right to report a concern to a government, licensing, or professional authority. Any payment, refund, cancellation, limitation, or dispute terms remain governed by the signed engagement agreement and applicable law.

### 4. Client response and electronic signature

The Client must choose one response and provide comments. For an acknowledgment, the Client may enter **No issue to report**. For an issue report, the Client should describe the specific deliverable, date, and requested follow-up without entering sensitive taxpayer or payment information.

By signing, the Client confirms that the response is the Client's own response, the listed completion record was reviewed, and the Client had an opportunity to report an issue. The electronic signature and DocuSeal audit record may be retained as evidence of delivery and response.
